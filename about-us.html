About The School

SJM Govt. Senior Secondary School Sohari is a senior secondary school. It is named after Shaheed Jitendra which is located in block Bijhari in disrict Hamirpur Himachal Pradesh. It is located in rural area. The school consists of grades from 6 to 12. The school is co educational and it doest not have an attached pre primary section. The school is approachable by all weather road. In this school academic session starts in month of April. The school has Govt. Building. It has 3 blocks. It has 18 rooms and a multi purpose hall and all the classrooms are in well condition. The school has well maintained ICT lab, Agriculture lab, Science Lab and Sports room.

The school has big library and all types of books for students to enhance their knowledge. The school has road safety club in which various activities are carried out time to time to aware the students about road safety. Besides this the school also has disaster management committee, eco club, school health and wellness, sanitization committee and Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat club in which different activities are carried out to aware the students. The school has MDM(Mid day Meal) facility for the students from 6th to 8th class. The school is under CCTV surveillance. The school provides scholarship to the students in different categories.

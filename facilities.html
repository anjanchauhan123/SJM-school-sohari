GSSS Infrastructure:-

PM SHRI school of excellence Govt. sen. sec. school Nadaun offers a modern and well-equipped infrastructure designed to support both learning and living. The campus includes spacious and separate hostels for boys, ensuring a comfortable stay for students. A large playground, beautifully surrounded by flower beds, provides ample space for outdoor activities. The school is also graced by a pine grove at the outskirts which adds to the natural charm of the campus. Located in a peaceful and picturesque setting, the school offers an ideal environment for students to thrive academically and personally. A very beautiful,well-equipped building will be ready soon.

DIGITAL CLASSROOMS

PM SHRI school of excellence Govt. sen. sec. school Nadaun ,Our school provides well-equipped learning laboratories that support modern and effective education. The Computer Lab helps students gain practical knowledge of computers, coding, research, and digital applications. The ICT Lab enhances learning through multimedia tools, internet access, and communication technologies, encouraging creativity and collaboration. Smart board–enabled classrooms make teaching interactive by using videos, animations, and visual presentations that improve understanding. These advanced facilities create an engaging learning environment, promote digital literacy, and help students develop critical thinking skills, preparing them for academic excellence and future technological challenges.

A.T.L :-

PM SHRI school of excellence Govt. sen. sec. school Nadaun The Atal Tinkering Lab (ATL) in our school is a dedicated space that promotes innovation, creativity, and hands-on learning among students. The lab provides opportunities to explore science, technology, engineering, and mathematics through practical activities such as robotics, coding, electronics, and model building. ATL encourages students to think critically, experiment with new ideas, and solve real-life problems in a creative way. By fostering curiosity, teamwork, and scientific temper, the Atal Tinkering Lab helps students develop essential skills for the future and inspires them to become confident innovators and lifelong learners.
